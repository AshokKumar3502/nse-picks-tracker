How the gap-up fade works
Stocks
The about 218 F&O stocks, checked at 9:20 AM every weekday
Rule
A stock that opens 3% or more above the previous NSE close goes on the list. Not used when NIFTY is under its 200-day average (it lost money there).
Paper plan
Sell short at the close of the 9:15 AM candle (the 9:20 price), buy back at the 3:15 PM price, 100 shares, after Zerodha intraday charges. Profit when the price falls.
Tested
2019 to 2026, from the opening price: about +0.47% a trade after charges, 57% winners, 6 of 8 years positive; weaker lately (+0.15% in 2024 to 2026) and it lost -0.78% a trade in the 18 sessions before it went live. Shorting weak or bearish stocks lost money in every kind of market, so they are not on this list.
Why paper only
A short can lose more than expected if the price keeps rising. The scoreboard judges it after 20 trades.

How the swing list is made
Agents
Swing intel (swing_intel.py) ranks every stock, the swing checklist (india-swing, swing.py) checks its top 20, the Judge (india-debate) reviews them, and the tested swing rule (india-swing-desk) adds its own names. Run automatically at 7:50 PM on weekdays.
Stocks it looks at
All NIFTY 500 stocks
Data
Daily, weekly and monthly charts, relative strength vs NIFTY and the sector, volume and delivery, results calendar, quarterly results, promoter and bulk deals, market regime
Rules
A 100-point score picks the top 20; only names that pass all 11 swing safety checks (trend, setup, not stretched, enough trading, no results in the hold, loss fits the budget and more) make the list. The tested rule adds names from strong, strengthening sectors when NIFTY is above its 200-day average.
Your plan
Buy at the close of the 9:15 AM five-minute candle on the first session after the list, 100 shares each. Checklist names: sell at the day 5 close. Tested-rule names: hold to the day 10 close (a Nov 2026 test found 10 days made about +1.1% a trade against +0.14% at 5 days, ahead of the market in 8 of 8 years; stops made it worse).

How the results-jump list is made
Agent
Results jump (india-results-drift, results_drift.py), run automatically at 7:50 PM on weekdays, then the Judge (india-debate)
Stocks it looks at
All NIFTY 500 stocks that published quarterly results since the last close
Data
NSE's own results filing times, NSE's daily price, volume and delivery files, NIFTY 50 for market health
Rules
On its results day the stock closed up 4% or more, on at least double its usual volume, near the day's high, and NIFTY 50 is above its 200-day average. A results fall (down 4% or more on double volume, closing near the low) goes on the "don't buy" list.
Tested
2019 to 2026, 642 jumps held 40 trading days: about +3.9% a trade after charges, 59% made money, about 2.7 points better than the market in both halves of the test (+2.2% at 20 days, only +0.2% at 5). Stops made it worse. 2020, 2022 and 2026 so far lost; 1 trade in 20 lost 20% or more, so keep each position small. Results falls lagged the market in all 7 years.
Your plan
Buy at the close of the 9:15 AM five-minute candle on the next session, hold up to 40 trading days (about 2 months), sell at the day 40 close, 100 shares each

How the 20-day hold list is made
Agent
Swing intel (swing_intel.py), run automatically at 7:50 PM on weekdays
Stocks it looks at
All NIFTY 500 stocks
Rules
The stock closes above its 50-day average, the 50-day is above the 200-day, it trades at least ₹5 crore a day, it is not on the avoid list (failed breakout, distribution, exhaustion), and NIFTY 50 is above its 200-day average; otherwise the list stands aside. The top 10 by swing-intel score are traded here.
Tested
NIFTY 500, 2017 to 2026, out of sample: about +1% a trade after delivery charges, held 20 days, but only 42% of trades made money, and buying any stock in an uptrend did just as well, so the gain is the market's drift, not the picking. Losing streaks were long and some trades gapped far through the stop: keep each position small.
Your plan
Buy at the close of the 9:15 AM five-minute candle on the next session, hold 20 trading days (about a month), sell at the day 20 close, 100 shares each. The stop shown is 2 ATR under the close; move it to 2 ATR under your real buy price.

How the scoreboard works
What it shows
The real record of every list since 17 Nov 2026, traded your way: bought at the close of the 9:15 AM candle, 100 shares, sold at 3:15 PM (intraday), after 5 or 10 days (swing), after 20 days (20-day hold) or after 40 days (results jump). Only finished trades count.
Compared with
The average stock bought and sold at the same times: every F&O stock from open to close for intraday, every NIFTY 500 stock from the buy day's open to the sell day's close for swing. "Beat the market by" is before charges on both sides, so it is a fair comparison.
When to trust it
A rule needs at least 20 finished trades before the scoreboard gives a verdict. "Beating the market" also needs a clear lead, not one lucky week (a t-value of 2 or more).
What happens next
A rule that lags the market after 20 or more trades gets fixed or dropped. A rule that beats it keeps its place.

How these lists are made and scored
Intraday list
The morning desk's bullish names, made before the 9:15 open from the previous close and overnight data, for that day only. Lists before 16 Nov 2026 were rebuilt later from each day's data, so they show no time.
Intraday plan
Buy every name at the close of the 9:15 AM five-minute candle, sell at the 3:15 PM price, 100 shares each, after Zerodha intraday charges.
Swing list
Made after the close: the names that passed every swing safety check, plus any from the tested swing rule. Checklist names are held 5 sessions, tested-rule names 10.
Judge
A fixed-rule check on each name: TAKE (no red flag), WAIT (3 or more warnings), SKIP (a hard red flag).
Swing plan
Buy at the close of the 9:15 AM five-minute candle on the first session after the list; each day's P/L is that day's close against the buy, on 100 shares; the final P/L sells at the day 5 close (checklist names) or day 10 close (tested-rule names), after delivery charges (STT, stamp, exchange, GST, DP).
