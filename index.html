How these lists are made and scored
Intraday list
The morning desk's bullish names, made before the 9:15 open from the previous close and overnight data, for that day only. Lists before 16 Nov 2026 were rebuilt later from each day's data, so they show no time.
Intraday result
One fixed breakout rule (above), 100 shares a trade, after Zerodha intraday charges; the buy and sell prices include a small slippage. "No breakout" means the rule never bought.
Swing list
Made after the close: the names that passed every swing safety check, plus any from the tested swing rule. Meant to be held 2 to 5 sessions.
Judge
A fixed-rule check on each name: TAKE (no red flag), WAIT (3 or more warnings), SKIP (a hard red flag).
Moves
From the entry (the next morning's open) to that close, before charges.
